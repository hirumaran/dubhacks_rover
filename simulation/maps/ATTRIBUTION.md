The original downloaded XML is included as `uw_campus.osm`, together with `provenance.json`, projection information and importer configuration. Coordinates describe the public UW campus simulation, not recorded telemetry from a real person or robot.

Building facades and tree canopies in the active world are procedural approximations. OSM footprints, pedestrian topology and mapped tree positions are retained. The official Webots importer output is preserved in `../worlds/uw_imported.wbt`.
